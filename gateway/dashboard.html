🔧 How Slide Panel Works

This dashboard has a slide panel on the right that you can open to see:

  • 🤖 What agents are running (name, model, persona, task, status, started time)
  • 📋 What tasks are doing (subagent, cron, multi-ai, custom_api, etc.)
  • 🧠 What AI models are in use (ollama/llama3.1:8b, groq/llama-3.1, etc.)
  • ✅ Recently completed

Panel auto-refreshes every 2 seconds when open. Bottom toolbar in TUI also shows live counts.

TUI: Type /panel or /agents to slide open panel in terminal + bottom toolbar shows live Agents: X | Tasks: Y
